Take control of your money without giving up your privacy.

Denaro is a private, offline personal finance app for tracking accounts, income, expenses, transfers, debts and recurring transactions in one place. No account is required, and your financial data stays encrypted on your device.

See the month at a glance
• Follow income, spending and cash flow from a clear dashboard
• Explore monthly trends and category breakdowns
• Filter your activity to find the transactions that matter

Organize finances your way
• Manage multiple accounts and currencies
• Create custom categories for income and expenses
• Record transfers between accounts
• Track money you owe or are owed, due dates and partial repayments
• Add recurring transactions and keep regular activity organized
• Hide balances whenever you want more privacy on screen

Made for everyday clarity
Denaro keeps your finances easy to review with a focused interface, light and dark themes, and no network permission.

Your money. Your device. Your data.
